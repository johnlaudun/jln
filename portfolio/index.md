# Portfolio

Academics and researchers produce a lot of material. While its inherent interest varies, the need to collect it -- either for the sake of sharing, for the sake of a vita, or because the producer is forgetful -- remains constant. After a lot of to-ing and fro-ing, I settled on collecting everything on GitHub, where they can be accessed either through this website or through the GitHub repository interface.

Not everything here is fully accessible: in addition to plain text files and PDFs, there are plenty of Word, Pages, and Keynote documents. If there is something here that interests you but you cannot open it for whatever reason, please contact me and let know what format works for you. 

In time as many document as possible will be in open, accessible formats. For now, I am still in the process of putting/pulling everything together. While I do that, please feel free to browse available [publications][], [presentations][], [papers][], [essays][], [appearances][], and [workshops][].
